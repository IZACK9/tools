How this is calculated

APR = rate × payments per day × 365. Funding collected = size × rate × payments per day × days. Fees = size × (spot fee + perp fee) × 2 (you open and close both legs).

Rough guide: 0.01% every 8h ≈ 11%/yr is "normal". Above ~0.05% every 8h (≈55%/yr) is a crowded, frenzied market. A negative rate means shorts pay longs, so this setup would pay instead of collect.

What this ignores: the rate changes every interval and can flip negative, exchange risk, withdrawal fees, borrow costs, and the price gap between spot and perp when you exit. Assumes the rate stays the same, which it won't.
