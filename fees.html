How this is calculated

Position = margin × leverage. Fee per trade = position × fee per side × 2 (you pay to open and to close). Fees per day = fee per trade × trades per day. Fees per month = fees per day × trading days.

Break-even move = fee per side × 2. Every trade starts that far in the red before price moves at all.

Typical taker fees on crypto perpetuals are around 0.04–0.06% per side; maker fees are lower. Check your own exchange's fee page. This ignores funding payments, spread and slippage, which add to the cost.
