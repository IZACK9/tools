Funding Rate Cheat Sheet

← Candle That Kills · one page, no hype

What it is

Perpetual futures never expire. To keep their price glued to the real (spot) price, the crowded side pays the other side every few hours. That payment is the funding rate.

Positive rate
Longs pay shorts. More people are betting up.
Negative rate
Shorts pay longs. More people are betting down.

Where to find it

  • Bybit: open a USDT perpetual (e.g. BTCUSDT). The top bar above the chart shows Funding Rate and a countdown.
  • Binance Futures: open the perpetual. The top bar shows Funding / Countdown.
  • OKX: open the perpetual swap. The top bar shows Funding rate and time to next payment.

Check the interval. Most pairs pay every 8 hours (3× a day), but some pay every 4h or 1h.

Turn it into a yearly number

APR = rate × payments per day × 365

Example: 0.01% every 8h → 0.01% × 3 × 365 = 10.95% / yr

Normal vs frenzy (8h rate)

Rate every 8h≈ per yearWhat it usually means
below 0%negativeFear. Shorts are crowded.
0.01%≈ 11%Default / normal market
0.03%≈ 33%Heated. Longs getting crowded.
0.05%≈ 55%Frenzy territory
0.10%+≈ 110%+Extreme. Historically short-lived.

The honest part

  • The rate changes every interval and can flip negative. Today's number is not next week's number.
  • In my own tests, holding spot + short perp to collect funding made roughly 20–30%/yr only in frenzies (2021, early 2024) and about 0–3%/yr in quiet markets.
  • Fees, exchange risk, and the spot–perp price gap at exit all eat into it. Check before you assume.
